Marks

A. The House W

A W whose middle peak is a roof, with the front door under it. The name and a home in one shape. Logo, app icon and favicon.

B. The Seal

For shirts, car magnets and the card left after each clean.

C. The Front Door

Alternate, unused unless Nick prefers it. Warm, says home, does not carry the name.

The line

Clean as a whistle, every other week.

The idiom tells people how to hear the name, and every other week is the product. Alternates: "Your house, clean as a whistle." and "Same team, same day, every other week."
