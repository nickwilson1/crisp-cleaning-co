Crisp Cleaning Co, brand v3

26 October 2026. Renamed from Whistle House Cleaning on the criterion that the name say clean for a house, an apartment and a business. Warm and plain. The Front Door is the logo and app icon, the seal is for print.

Marks

The Front Door

An arched door with a brass fanlight and knob on a porch green tile. Any door, a house, an apartment or an office. Logo, app icon and favicon.

The Seal

For shirts and the card left after each clean.

The door alone

The mark without its tile, for print on linen or green.

The line

Crisp. Every other week.

The name is the result and every other week is the product. Alternates: "Your place, crisp, from the same two cleaners." and "Same team, same day, every other week."
